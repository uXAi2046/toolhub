Level 1: The "Pizza-Poetry" Neuron

Imagine if a single neuron in your brain fired only when you saw your grandmother. That would be easy to understand.

Level 2: The Prism (Sparse Autoencoders)

So, how do we untangle this mess? Researchers use a technique called a Sparse Autoencoder (SAE).

Think of the neuron's activity as a "musical chord" made of many notes played at once. The SAE acts like a prism (or Fourier transform) that splits the chord back into individual notes (features).
